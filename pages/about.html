Who We Are

About FixKart

FixKart is a hardware marketplace and home services platform designed to make everyday repairs, maintenance and improvement simpler.

Instead of searching for hardware products in one place and reliable professionals in another, FixKart brings both together through a single platform.

Customers can browse hardware products, discover useful tools and supplies, and book professionals for services such as plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, painting, AC repair and automotive assistance.

Our goal is to create a convenient and reliable experience where customers can get the products they need and find the right professional for the job.
