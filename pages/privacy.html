Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how FixKart collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use FixKart, we may collect information that you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, service address, and account information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve FixKart services, process hardware orders, arrange professional services, communicate with you, and provide customer support.

3. Orders and Bookings

Information related to your orders and service bookings may be used to process transactions, arrange deliveries, connect you with professionals, and provide order or booking updates.

4. Account Information

If you create a FixKart account, you are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. You should notify us if you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Payment Information

Payment information may be processed through appropriate payment services. FixKart should not unnecessarily store sensitive payment credentials such as complete card information.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

FixKart may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve website functionality, and understand how users interact with the platform.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Sharing of Information

We may share necessary information with service professionals, delivery partners, payment providers, or other service providers when required to complete an order or booking.

9. Your Choices

You may review or update certain account information through your FixKart profile. You may also contact us regarding questions about your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

FixKart is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page along with the updated policy date where applicable.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the FixKart team.
